Frequently Asked FAQs

How long does the AICTE approval process take?
Our compliance engine evaluates PDFs and formats instantly. Board evaluator validation inspections generally process within 3-5 business days.
What documents are required for application?
Key files needed: Land Ownership/Lease Certificate, Certified Building Blueprint, Fire Safety Audit, and Bharat Bill Fee Receipt. All files must be official PDFs.
How does AI document verification work?
Our system runs OCR text reading, verifies legal stamps, extracts building plans scale, and cross-references faculty qualification credentials automatically.
How can I reset my security password?
For security purposes, password changes must be authorized by institution directors. Please submit a request ticket to helpdesk@aicte.gov.in.
